# Milestone 029 — Grouped job-and-method attention

## Authorization and scope

Matt authorized one bounded production change on 2026-09-16 after holding three experiments. The
authorization superseded milestone 028's one-target/six-concrete-candidate rule only when the
responsibility branch presents exactly two eligible known tribute-refusal targets. Scope was
recorded in `CURRENT_MILESTONE.md` and the exception in `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md` before runtime work.

The approved representation retains up to six top-level alternatives containing at most ten
concrete leaves. A surviving job group consumes one top-level slot and contains its independently
salient methods. No job or other category has a reserved slot: two retained groups leave at most
four singleton slots, while fewer groups allow more singletons to compete. Eligibility, evidence,
ordering, cancellation priority, late feasibility and player-facing candidate-id order are those
falsified by the held grouped experiment.

Traits, weights, thresholds, information routing, B/C work, UI or presentation, earlier feasibility,
backfill, protected slots, dominance pruning, generic hierarchy, other action families, more than
two target groups, persistent decision phases and RNG redesign were excluded. The milestone makes
no claim that the two jobs are meaningfully different or non-dominated plans.

## Implemented behavior

- The existing known-refusal responsibility generator admits the first two eligible targets in
  belief order and retains all three existing methods for each. Its unattributed-shortfall fallback
  remains a one-target occasion.
- Group activation is determined after redundancy and before salience from exactly two distinct
  candidate families whose leaves are generated by `FromResponsibility`, start
  `SecureTribute`, name a target, use `Persuade`, `Threaten` or `Force`, and require the exact
  `BusinessRefusesTribute(TargetId)` claim. Every non-activating occasion takes the literal original
  flat six-candidate path.
- Salience remains leaf-specific. Only surviving leaves form a group; an empty group disappears and
  reserves no slot. Group identity is `(StartStrategy, SecureTribute, TargetId)`. The strongest
  surviving leaf is the anchor, with candidate id breaking equal-salience ties.
- Focused cancellation remains a singleton and sorts first. Other top-level alternatives sort by
  salience descending and then ordering id ordinal. Up to the first six survive. Leaves of crowded-out
  alternatives are rejected at salience; retained leaves return to the original flat
  cancellation/salience/id order before feasibility and RNG-consuming utility evaluation.
- Knowledge, capability and access feasibility remain late and do not backfill a lost slot.
  Player-facing options remain concrete leaves in ordinal candidate-id order. Resolution and commit
  still select one existing leaf.
- The allocation record is preparation-only metadata. Controlled and autonomous actors share the
  same generator, filters, scores, concrete identities and commit path; no group key or new phase is
  saved.
